Support

Help for the ClearSubs iOS app, published by Andrii Svitelskyi.

Questions, bug reports or feedback: email andriy.svitelskyi@gmail.com. Write in English or Ukrainian. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened. Do not send screenshots that show information you want to keep private.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. ClearSubs has no account and no server that receives your subscriptions. Deleting the app removes the data. Use Settings › Export as JSON to keep a copy first.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in ClearSubs and tap Restore Purchases. The one-time purchase is tied to your Apple Account, not to the device.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I cancel a subscription I track in the app?

ClearSubs only tracks subscriptions; it cannot cancel them. Open the item and use the cancellation help link, or manage subscriptions bought through Apple in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone.

My reminders do not arrive.

Check that notifications are allowed for ClearSubs in Settings › Notifications and that reminders are switched on in the app’s Settings. Reminders are created on your device, so they do not need an internet connection.

The scanner did not find my subscriptions.

Use a clear, uncropped screenshot of the Subscriptions list or a bill, and check the review screen before adding. Scanning runs on your device and nothing is saved until you confirm. You can always add an item by hand.
