ClearSubs Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-29 · Last updated: 2026-10-29

1. Who we are

ClearSubs (bundle identifier com.andriisvitelskyi.ClearSubs) is an iOS application published by Andrii Svitelskyi (“we”, “us”), an individual developer. This policy explains what personal data the app handles, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to the app only, not to any other product or website.

For data protection purposes, Andrii Svitelskyi is the controller of any personal data described below. You can reach us at andriy.svitelskyi@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • Everything you enter in the app stays on your device. We have no account system and no server that receives it.
  • The app makes a few small network requests for public files. They are the same for every user and contain no information about you or your subscriptions (see section 6).
  • We have no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.
  • Scanning a screenshot or a photo is done on your device. The image is not uploaded.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

  • Content you create — subscription names, amounts, currencies, billing dates, notes, categories and links you add. This is stored only on your device (section 5).
  • Support messages — if you email us, we receive your address and whatever you write.

3.2 Information collected automatically

We do not collect any information automatically. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting code. The network requests it makes are listed in section 6.

3.3 Information from third parties

Apple tells the app whether the one-time purchase has been made, so that it can unlock the paid feature. We do not buy or receive personal data about you from anyone.

4. Device permissions

The app asks for a permission only when a feature needs it, and iOS shows you the reason at that moment. You can withdraw any permission in Settings › ClearSubs; the feature that relied on it stops working and the rest of the app keeps working.

PermissionWhy the app asks
Camera To take a photo of a bill or subscription screen so the app can pre-fill a new entry. The photo is read on your device and is not sent anywhere.
Face ID To lock the app when you turn on App Lock. The app never sees your biometric data; iOS only tells it whether authentication succeeded.
Notifications To remind you before a renewal or the end of a trial. These are local notifications created on your device. The app does not use push notifications and no notification content is sent to a server.

Choosing a screenshot from your photo library uses the system photo picker, which gives the app only the pictures you select; the app does not ask for access to your whole library.

5. Data that stays on your device

The subscriptions and settings you create in ClearSubs are stored locally on your device. We cannot read them, we do not receive a copy of them, and they are removed when you delete the app. A Home Screen or Lock Screen widget reads a copy of the same data from storage shared with the app on your device. If you use iCloud device backups, a copy may be included in that backup under Apple’s terms and encryption, not ours. If you use Export, the app writes a file that you choose where to share or save; we do not receive it.

Reading a subscription from a screenshot or photo uses text recognition and, where your device supports Apple Intelligence, Apple’s on-device language model. Both run on your device.

6. Network requests the app makes

These are the only times the app contacts the internet, apart from purchases handled by Apple (section 8). None of them includes your subscriptions, amounts, notes or any account identifier. The services involved can see your IP address, as any web request allows.

RequestPurposeWhat is sentProvider and policy
Exchange rates To convert amounts between currencies. The app downloads a public rates file and caches it for about a day. Only the request for a public file of rates (USD). It contains none of your data. Public currency-api data (CC0), delivered by jsDelivr (policy) and Cloudflare Pages (policy)
Service logos To show the logos of the services in the app’s built-in catalog. The app downloads the whole set once, after it first opens, and keeps it on your device. The website domains of the built-in catalog, for example netflix.com, to our own logo host. Every user’s app requests the same list, so what you add or track never changes what is requested. Our logo host, served by Cloudflare Pages (policy)
Logo fallback If our logo host has no image for a catalog domain, the app asks a public icon service once. Nothing you type is ever sent. The catalog website domain only. DuckDuckGo (policy)

A logo that could not be downloaded is retried after about seven days. These requests are made for everyone and do not depend on what you track. We do not use them to identify you or to build a profile of you.

8. Purchases

The app offers one optional one-time purchase that removes the free-plan limit. It is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment and receives your payment details; the app learns only whether the purchase has been made, which is what lets it unlock the feature and restore it on a new device. Apple’s handling of your payment data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. We never receive your card number or billing details.

9. Tracking and advertising

The app contains no advertising SDKs, does not read Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA), does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not build advertising profiles. Because of that, the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

10. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Because we hold no personal data from the app, there is nothing for us to disclose beyond the requests in section 6. We would disclose data we hold only when the law requires it, to protect someone’s safety, or to a successor if the app changes hands, in which case this policy continues to apply until you are told otherwise.

11. International transfers

The providers in section 6 may process requests outside your country, including in the United States. Because those requests contain no personal data beyond a technical IP address, we do not transfer your content anywhere. Ask us at andriy.svitelskyi@gmail.com if you want details about a given provider.

12. How long we keep data

We do not keep personal data from the app on any server. Data held only on your device lives there until you delete it or delete the app. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to answer you and then deleted.

13. Security

Traffic between the app and the services in section 6 is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). On your device the app’s data is protected by iOS file protection, and you can add App Lock with Face ID or your passcode. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep the amount of data we hold as small as the app allows. That is the strongest protection we can offer.

14. Your rights and choices

  • Access — ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix data that is wrong.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your data.
  • Objection and restriction — object to processing based on legitimate interests, or ask us to pause it.
  • Portability — receive a copy of the data you gave us in a machine-readable format.

The data you enter in the app is on your device, so you can view, change, export or delete it yourself in the app, or remove it all by deleting the app. For anything we might hold, such as a support email, write to andriy.svitelskyi@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify that the request comes from you.

If you are in the EEA or the UK and you think we have handled your data wrongly, you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

15. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email andriy.svitelskyi@gmail.com and we will delete it.
